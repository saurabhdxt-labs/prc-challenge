# Fresh review: the path to 246

Reviewed against the saved v6/v7 artifacts and fold predictions on September 10, 2026.
The latest locally recorded board score is **285.8013**, from v7. The live score could
not be refreshed. This is a research plan, with no newly fitted candidate or claimed gain.

**Recommendation:** spend the next modeling slots on native categorical modeling,
a schedule-relative regression target, and correctly conditioned fill/body experts.
Measure their combined predictions against v7. The existing evidence does not establish
a path to 246; it identifies concrete experiments capable of testing a larger improvement.

The exact gap is **285.8013² − 246² = 21,166.38 MSE**, or **25.91%** of current MSE.
A collection of 300–1,000 MSE improvements is too small for this objective.

ranking file's matched-row proportion. These are **weighted fold error budgets**, not
observed components of the hidden score or promised recoverable gains.

| Disjoint matched subset | Rows | Weighted fold MSE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Schedule-fill: `abs(y − sp) <= 60 s` | 30,167 | 7,148 |

Rome contains 4,435 of the 7,148 fill MSE. For the non-fill clock-disagreement tail,

Even perfect predictions on all matched Rome rows remove only 10,724 weighted fold

Neither calculation is a bound on other modeling work.

**2. The strongest untested representation: native categorical models with two targets.**

The existing `scripts/catboost_fold_worker.py` requires a float32 matrix and supplies
no `cat_features`. It does not test CatBoost's native handling of identities and their
combinations, which is a separate mechanism described in the
[official documentation](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/features/categorical-features).

Run one full-volume development seed with native categorical inputs: airport, scoped
stand/runway, full flight/callsign plus coarse airline/operator, route and aircraft type.
Keep useful numeric clocks, queue and ordering features. Preserve coarse fallbacks for
unseen identities. A poor single-key mean is not a test of these joint representations.

Fit the first model to the current target:

```
delta = BLOCK − AOBT
y_hat = max((MVT − AOBT) − delta_hat, 1)
```

The second, previously unimplemented target is:

```
schedule_delay = BLOCK − SCHED = sp − y
y_hat = max(sp − schedule_delay_hat, 1)
```

Schedule fills become values near zero in the second target. The supplied schedule
offset is retained exactly in reconstruction. This tests the same useful principle as
the original delta reparameterization, using a different reference clock. It could
perform worse on ordinary delays, so it is an alternative expert, with full squared
loss and all labels retained. It adds no new information by itself; the hypothesis is
that the representation is easier for the learner. The earlier direct-y experiment
does not test this target.

Keep feature inputs and model settings matched for the target comparison. Train
blending/routing weights on inner out-of-fold predictions, never on the scored holdout.
The registered numeric CatBoost arm can retain its original definition; give this
native-input comparison its own registration before fitting.

**3. Complete the missing conditional body, and select the mixture by squared error.**

The prior fill experiments mixed a fill prediction with a model already trained on
both fills and non-fills. They therefore did not test a correctly conditioned mixture.
`scripts/rome_body.py` has only a smoke artifact; its full-fit result is absent.
`scripts/cond_experts.py` implements the broader conditioned design.

Use `E[y | X, non-fill]` for the body, `sp + small fitted correction` for the fill
branch, and an estimated fill probability. Include Rome. Evaluate the all-airport
variant as well as Rome's contribution; do not make the entire program Rome-only.

For a new follow-up, select the classifier's stopping point and any calibration/blend
against **the assembled taxi prediction's squared error on inner validation**. AUC
alone ignores whether a probability error moves the prediction by 30 seconds or 20,000.
Use continuous probabilities as the primary mixture. A fixed `p >= 0.5` gate is one
specific fallback, not the general optimum for this regression task.

The all-rows baseline can be retained as another expert, with its blend learned from
inner out-of-fold predictions. Do not interpret the existing baseline as a non-fill
conditional expectation. Report fill and non-fill losses separately, but recognize that
the existing hard body-loss veto is a conservative experiment requirement, not the
competition objective. Preserve verdicts under the old registrations; preregister any
new tradeoff policy rather than changing those verdicts retroactively.

A cheap diagnostic from existing records: choosing the best prediction **using the true
label** among F, direct-y, weather, schedule and raw NM proxy removes **28,584** weighted
fold MSE. This is not a learned gate or a forecast. It shows prediction disagreements
worth testing, while a bad gate can lose enormously: the schedule predictor alone is
very poor. No label-oracle gain belongs in the candidate ledger, and separate expert
oracle gains must not be added. Inner OOF routing followed by independent validation
is the test of whether these disagreements can actually be used.

**4. Reprice the unmatched work and avoid claims about the leader's method.**

v7's Rome change removed **630 board MSE**, against roughly 4,150 projected from fold A.
That does not demonstrate a general 15% transfer factor, but it does invalidate treating
that particular projection as a reliable forecast. The July public-model comparison
was a clue about one validation month, not a decomposition of the board gap.

After splicing R2 into the stored unmatched fold, **two LFPG rows account for 60.76% of
its remaining SSE**. The resulting complete fold RMSE is 312.53, against the recorded
board 285.80. Those absolute totals are different instruments; do not select a model
by pretending one forecasts the other.

After the matched candidates, evaluate a native categorical non-fill model and fill
mixture on the unmatched rows across all twelve leave-one-month-out folds. Preserve
training-supported extreme branches, but require improvement across dates and months,
and report how much of the gain comes from the largest one, two and five errors.
The official full-tail score remains the objective; these cuts diagnose fragility.

A competitor's aggregate score jump cannot identify which rows changed or prove their
method. No proposed experiment here uses scores to infer hidden labels.

**5. What a 246-sized result would have to look like.**

The ranking proportions are known, so the identity is exact:

```
total_MSE = 0.9846596 × matched_RMSE² + 0.0153404 × unmatched_RMSE²
```

The following are **target combinations on the hidden scoring set**, not measured
component scores or forecasts. Each gives total RMSE 246:

| Matched RMSE target | Unmatched RMSE required |
|---:|---:|
| 205 | 1,116.9 |
| 195 | 1,226.4 |
| 190 | 1,275.8 |
| 185 | 1,322.1 |

If, only for planning, the current hidden matched RMSE equals the measured 222.563,
the 190/1,275.8 route needs about **13,228 matched MSE + 7,938 unmatched MSE** recovered.
The current hidden unmatched error is not separately observed. Paired matched gains
transferred well in four past submissions; this is evidence for prioritizing that
component, not proof that its absolute fold RMSE transfers or future gains will do so.
With the same assumption and no unmatched gain, matched RMSE would need **167.45**.

**6. Concrete execution order and decision rules.**

1. **Prepare one reusable reference.** Freeze v7 and stable row IDs. Keep January/July
   as inspected development data. Inventory previously used months and lock the least
   used confirmation split; if no untouched months remain, call it replication rather
   than a fresh holdout. Add a forward-time stress test. Repair the stopping encoder
   in the new runner and eventual submission path. The existing repair also changes
   refit encodings: either isolate that change or report its joint effect. Do not bank
   any gain before measuring it against F.
2. **Run the substantive candidates.** Complete the conditioned body if already running;
   otherwise prioritize the native categorical delta model, then the matched-feature
   schedule-delay target comparison, then the conditioned mixture. Use one full-volume
   seed for development; this is screening, not confirmation. Add a one-seed mode where
   needed rather than mislabeling a 60-tree smoke run. Run one heavy job at a time.
3. **Shortlist using combined net MSE.** Retain a candidate for expensive confirmation
   if it contributes at least 1,000 weighted fold MSE alone or marginally in an inner-OOF
   ensemble. The number is a compute-allocation rule. A smaller result is not a refuted
   hypothesis. Do not add separately reported gains from overlapping candidates.
4. **Confirm the assembled prediction.** Three seeds, paired uncertainty resampled by
   date, airport and January/July cuts, replication months, and a forward-time check.
   Save per-row predictions and feature/split/target manifests so gates and blends do
   not require repeated base fits. Price unmatched gains separately with all-month
   evidence and error-concentration diagnostics.
5. **Use the result to decide the next research slot.** A 5,000-MSE gain maps to 276.92
   only under one-to-one transfer; 10,000 maps to 267.74; even 20,000 maps to 248.36.
   A confirmed gain of only 1,000–3,000 does not validate a path to 246. It calls for a
   new source of predictive information or a materially different model, not repeated
   seed/weather/order refits presented as gap closure. A 246 claim requires a complete
   candidate with evidence at the full 21,166-MSE scale and ultimately a scored result.

The next decisive deliverable is a table of **F versus native-category delta versus
schedule-delay versus conditioned mixture versus their validated ensemble**, with
disjoint error contributions. That will establish whether the remaining gap responds
to representation and conditioning. The current record has not yet run that test.
