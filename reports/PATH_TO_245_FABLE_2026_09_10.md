# The path to 245 — an outside review, 2026-09-10 (afternoon)

**Question answered:** how can the board go from 285.8013 (v7) to 245 (leader 245.02)?
**Verdict up front:** on current evidence 245 is **not reachable** by the end of the competition
(honest probability ≈ 3%). The 21,657 MSE gap cannot be closed in the matched lane by any
realistic model, and the only lane large enough to hold it — the unmatched lane, ≈ 31–33k of our
81,682 board MSE, concentrated in a few hundred LIRF rows — is a per-row fill / date-slip
classification problem for which no serve-time information beyond what we already use was found.
Two cheap, evidence-backed moves do exist (≈ +5,600 expected from two LIRF rows; ≈ +1–2k from a
physically grounded disruption-hour term) and, with arm B, put **274 at roughly a coin flip**;
top 5 (266.4) at ≈ 15%.

Every number below carries its source. Nothing was fitted; no cache was built; no submission was
touched; the leaderboard was not used. Scratch scripts are listed in §7 and reproduce every figure.
Compute: read-only parquet passes, ≤ 1.5 GB, all under two minutes each, `nice -n 19`, while arm B
(pid 52010) kept the heavy slot.

Conventions obeyed: BC-2 (arm F's record is DELTA, recovered as `max(proxy − treatment, 1)`,
reproduces 222.56316 to 1e-10; `stratum_fold_v7_preds` and `rome_dateslip_preds` are taxi times).
One convention the handoff does not state and that cost me a run: **`row` in the fold records indexes
the twelve-month concatenation of `data/cache_stand/training_2025-*.parquet` in filename order
(max 1,183,378), not the two holdout months.** Verified by y/proxy/airport equality on all 339,015
rows (`scratch/hour_oracle.py`). BC-2's map should record it.

## 5. What the project has wrong, or mispriced

1. **R2 shipped without a 2026-composition expectation, and it moved the two highest-stake rows the
   wrong way.** Under 2025 band rates R2 was ≈ 0 in expectation before upload (§2 P7); the board's
   −630 is consistent with that, not with a mysterious 0.15× transfer factor. R2 lowered sp 39,365 →
   4,524 and sp 33,057 → 6,196 (v6 already had them at 10,928 / 12,904) although 2025 has 20 fills
   in 20 rows in that band, 14 of 14 at dayoff = 0. Root cause: the stake-weighted classifier is
   structurally under-confident on 10–15% of high-sp fills out of sample (LOMO: p_R < 0.5 on 10 of
   96 fills with sp ≥ 4 h), and airline / hour effects that are right at 2–4 h are applied where the
   sp band already decides. A monotone floor (E1) is the structural fix; a recalibration is a patch.
2. **"Unmatched fold projections do not transfer" is over-generalised from one shipment.** The 2026
   LIRF regime looks like 2025 (arrival fill rates 10.8 / 12.3% vs 11.0 / 10.5%; sp composition of
   the 383 rows like fold A's). What failed was pricing on a fold-A row draw (Kish n ≈ 23). The rule
   should be: **price unmatched lanes on the 2026 file's composition under 2025 class rates**
   (`scratch/reprice_lirf.py` does it in seconds) — and treat that as the projection.
3. **The EHAM January 2026 disruption is outside 2025's support** (§3) and the closure that covered
   "the EHAM surge" did not test the quantity at risk (unmatched non-fill taxi-out in disrupted
   hours). Bounded at ≈ 1–2k, but unpriced and physically real; it is also the one place where the
   arrival records in the scored file are a legitimate serve-time witness.
4. **The plan's framing of the matched lane.** "The first two subsets contain enough error to cover
   the gap arithmetically" is true and misleading: covering it takes 87% of fill + tail error or 100%
   of ordinary error, both oracle-level. The correct sentence is that **the matched lane cannot
   reach 246 under any realistic model, and the unmatched lane's ≈ 31–33k is where the gap lives.**
   `WHERE_WE_STAND`'s retraction of the "≈ 250 ceiling" was right in form (an oracle sum is not a
   bound) but the surviving load-bearing number — the unmatched lane at ≈ 33k on the board — is
   corroborated here from the 2026 file itself (≥ 12.5k from 43 LIRF rows in the best case; 35.6k
   expected under band rates for all 383).
5. **Priority 3 (joint context, five-departure neighbourhoods) should be deprioritised**: the
   airport-hour and runway-hour label oracles are 1.8–2.6k (§1b) against a permuted control of −1.1k.
6. **Conventions.** `row` = index into the twelve-month cache concatenation (add to BC-2). The arm B
   fit log's "seed 0 alone: matched RMSE 232.1959 on 339,015 holdout rows" is an all-rows number
   for a body-only model (fill rows 373.5); quoting it without the fill / body split will read as a
   regression. The registered clause 4 is body rows only, where seed 0 is *better* than F.
7. **Nothing here revises a registered verdict.** RESULT R, DS, M, G, C stand as written. E1 and E3
   are new hypotheses and need their own pre-registration before a number is read as a result.
