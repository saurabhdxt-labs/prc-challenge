# Pipeline to 245 — error map, staged design, new-information hunt, honest bottom line (2026-09-10, 16:45)

**Question.** Design a complete, evidence-backed pipeline from the board's **281.5182 (v10, 79,252 MSE)** to **245.0207
(60,035 MSE)** — a gap of **19,217 MSE** — where every component is sound, measured and transferable; and say honestly what
is and is not reachable.

**Answer in one paragraph.** Measured on the fold records with every known number reproduced first (§0), v10's error is
≈ 49–51k in the matched lane (broad, no regime bias left: arm F's mean residual is within −19…+2 s in every
airport-hour congestion bin, including the hot ones) and ≈ 28–30k in the unmatched lane. Of the unmatched lane, only
≈ 14k is *mechanism* (ordinary rows in hot hours ≈ 4–6k; LIRF fills and ordinaries ≈ 8.6k); the rest — and by the board's
own arithmetic ≈ 14–16k of it was realised in 2026 — is **rare-class rows** (24 h date-slips, Family-B monsters) that
every serve-time test in this project, including six new ones today, fails to identify. Every measurable stage below is
priced event-excluded on the 2026 composition; their credible sum is **≈ +4k (range 1.5–8k)**, which puts **top 10 at
≈ 55%**, **top 5 at ≈ 12%** and **245 at ≈ 2%**. 245 needs ≈ 15k that has no located mechanism. The three experiments to run
first are E3R (registered, cheap), the ADS-B measurement of the EHAM storm days (the only *independent* instrument available
for the largest 2026-specific stake, and validatable on matched rows' `AOBT_3` before it touches a prediction), and the
CatBoost-native / BC-1 / capacity confirmation as one matched-lane program with inner-OOF blending.

Compute: read-only parquet passes, ≤ 0.66 GB RSS, ≤ 25 s each, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 nice -n 19`; one external HTTP request
(GitHub API, asset sizes). No fit over 2 min, no cache build, no submission, no board reading beyond the ledger's totals.
Scripts: `…/scratchpad/pipeline245/{matched_map,unm_map,stack,structure,flightid,raw_agg,hunt,lirf_combine,lirf_feats,eham}.py`
with `.out` files beside them.

### Stage 2 — ADS-B measurement of the EHAM storm days (the independent instrument)

- **Hypothesis:** for EHAM departures on 2–9 Jan 2026, the last ADS-B sample inside the flight's own stand gate is an
  off-block measurement with RMSE ≤ 250 s on ≥ 60% of rows, and it beats v10 (and E3R) on the unmatched storm rows.
- **Mechanism (built already):** `scripts/adsb_extract.py --day YYYY-MM-DD` streams one adsb.lol daily archive (ODbL-1.0)
  through `tarfile` without untarring to disk (peak ≈ 3.5 GiB), keeps only samples inside the ten airport boxes, deletes
  the archive. Confirmed today by one GitHub API request: `v2026.01.05-planes-readsb-prod-0` = 2 assets, **3.08 GB**.
  Eight days ≈ 25 GB streamed, ≈ 15 MB/day retained; disk has 61 GiB free (94% full) — sufficient with the delete-after
  discipline. Stand centroids: leave-one-flight-out from the 2026 file's *matched* EHAM departures (their `AOBT_3` is the
  off-block anchor; 2025 census: 230 of 312 stands had ≥ 2 fixes on one day — eight days give many more).
- **Join for unmatched rows (no callsign in the row):** runway + take-off second. Take-off from ADS-B = first sample with
  ground speed > 80 kt on the runway heading, or first airborne sample minus a fixed offset; `MVT_TIME_UTC_mvt` is the
  take-off second on the row. Opus measured runway + take-off *minute* unique on 99.86% of EHAM runway-minutes; the
  second-level join must be re-measured and its ambiguity rate recorded before use (clause C0).
- **The closure oracle is in the file:** EHAM's 2026 *matched* storm rows (≈ 2,500 on 2–9 Jan; 845 on 3–6 Jan alone) have
  `AOBT_3`. Sensor pushback vs `AOBT_3` gives bias and RMSE per stand per day *on 2026 data, in the storm*, before any
  unmatched prediction is touched. EHAM's `delta = BLOCK − AOBT_3` has per-row RMSE ≈ 160 s (3,106 wMSE / 40,141 rows), so
  `AOBT_3` is a tight external anchor. Fills are 0 of 705 at EHAM (2025), so the physics predicts the label directly.
- **Price (incremental over stage 1):** unmatched: 133 hot rows plus the 376 others; per-row residual after a level fix ≈
  800–1,500 s (matched spread) → replacing it with a 150–300 s measurement on 65% of rows ≈ **+0.8 to +1.8k**; matched
  EHAM storm rows: F's hot-hour RMSE ≈ 330 s (1,500–2,000 bin, 356 rows, 113 wMSE) rising in the storm; ADS-B at ≤ 250 s on
  65% ≈ +0.3–0.6k. Total **+1.0 to +2.5k**. P(works in 2026) 0.5 — snow may degrade ground reception, and 2025's census is
  one day.
- **Heavy hours:** ≈ 2–3 h streaming (8 × 15–20 min, network-bound) + ≈ 1 day of engineering; run at night, AC power, not in
  the model slot. **Dependencies:** after stage 1 ships (same rows; ADS-B overrides E3R only where the gate fires).
  **Licence:** ODbL share-alike on the derived table — document in the repo before shipping.
- **Prereg skeleton (locked before the first archive is fetched):** C0 second-level join ambiguity ≤ 2%; C1 on matched storm
  rows, sensor − `AOBT_3` bias-corrected RMSE ≤ 250 s and coverage ≥ 50% (else NOT WORKING, no unmatched row touched); C2 a
  leave-one-day-out check that the gate's RMSE on day d holds when centroids come from the other seven days; C3 the shipped
  rule replaces the prediction only where the gate fires *and* the sensor's day-RMSE ≤ 250; TRUE shape: board Δ ≤ −800 vs
  the stage-1 entry; FALSE: Δ ≥ 0.

### Stage 3 — matched-lane representation program (one registered program, inner-OOF blended)

- **Components:** (a) BC-1 repair wired into `lgbm_submit.py` (site 3) — a correctness gate, price ≈ 0; (b) capacity
